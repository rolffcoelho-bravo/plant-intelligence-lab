### B17 / B17-T1 — structural background, not novelty

For the additive model

\[
\widehat y(g,e)=\widehat b_0+z_G(g)^\top\widehat\beta_G+z_E(e)^\top\widehat\beta_E,
\]

the same-environment genotype contrast satisfies

\[
\widehat y(g_i,e)-\widehat y(g_j,e)
=[z_G(g_i)-z_G(g_j)]^\top\widehat\beta_G.
\]

The environmental main effect cancels in exact arithmetic. This explains why the deployed additive architecture cannot make genotype contrasts environment-dependent, but the result is standard structural algebra rather than new theory.

Terminal status:

`B17_T1_ARCHITECTURE_CONTRACTION_NOVELTY_REJECTED_TERMINATE_B17`

## 4. Publication claim boundary

### Primary claims permitted

1. The repository executed a **seal-first blinded external-validation protocol relative to repository outcome access** for the frozen 2024 target.
2. Source compatibility was treated as a formal precondition to prediction rather than repaired after reveal.
3. Missing official keys were preserved as missing-source evidence rather than silently deleted from the original seal.
4. The frozen `G+E_T1` predictor generalized nontrivially but modestly on the 779-cell external 2024 cohort.
5. The inherited frozen 90% interval rule satisfied the 2024 calibration criterion, while the predeclared one-sided carry-forward widening rule over-covered and had worse interval score.
6. The previous observed calibration-error direction was not sufficiently transport-stable to justify that particular one-sided next-season correction.
7. Postoutcome diagnosis showed a mixed failure structure, including environment offsets, within-environment residual error, weak-to-moderate genotype ordering, and severe response-spread compression.
8. The additive architecture cannot express environment-dependent genotype contrasts through its environmental main-effect term.
9. Hostile novelty audits terminated B15, B17, and B18 when proposed contributions collided with existing theory or primary prior art.
